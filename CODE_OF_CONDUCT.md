# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1. This is a short statement of it. The full text there applies.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and respectful, and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an address, without their permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a person. To report a problem, use a private message to the maintainer through their GitHub profile, or the private vulnerability reporting of this repository if the matter is also a security one. Reports are handled in confidence.
